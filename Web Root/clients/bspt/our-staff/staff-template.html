PERSON

DEGREES

Location: Bay State Physical Therapy – Somerville, MA

Graduate: Ithaca College

Nestor graduated from Ithaca College where he earned his Doctorate of Physical Therapy in addition to earning his B.S. in Clinical Health Studies with a minor in Business Administration. Nestor has developed his clinical skills in multiple outpatient orthopedic clinics, specializing in treating post-operative and sports-related injuries. He has utilized both land and aquatic treatment methods to help patients achieve their goals. He values using a combination of evidence-based practice and in-depth patient centered evaluation to create an individualized plan of care to maximize overall function.

Nestor was born in El Salvador and lived there for the first eight years of his life. He then moved to Cambridge and is an alumnus of Cambridge Rindge and Latin. While at Ithaca College, Nestor was the president of the club golf team, while also participating on the club volleyball and soccer teams. Outside of work, Nestor’s interests include golfing, soccer, trying new restaurants, and watching sports.

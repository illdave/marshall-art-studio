David M!

"Yaba-daba DON'T"

Location: Your hearts and minds

Graduate: Massachusetts College of Art & Design

David is rehabbing atrophied quads and connective tissue on both knees from a bicycle accident in early March. When a pothole knocked his feet off the pedals, he fractured both tibial plateaus trying to stop with his feet on the ground like Fred Flintstone. Doctors recommended letting them heal without surgery, which meant no weight bearing on either leg for at least three weeks. Recovery began at the Medford Rehabilitation Center and Nursing Home. He was wheelchair bound with leg braces for his first three weeks, then cleared for full weight bearing on one leg (transitioning from wheelchair to walker). He was discharged two weeks later after getting cleared for weight bearing on both legs.

Since getting assessed by Nestor on May 5th, David's been under Leslie's gentle care.

David has been an urban cyclist since childhood. His daily bike commutes were from home (Allston, Roxbury, Brighton, Camberville) to downtown Boston (Back Bay, South End, Seaport). Fun destinations include Waltham, Bedford, North and South shores. Life away from pedals includes web design & development, making comic books, teaching art, and obsession with weird British television (Doctor Who, The Sandbaggers).
